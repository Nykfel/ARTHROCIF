The primary search was performed using this reference database containing amino acid and nucleotide sequences from 14 bacterial *cifA* and *cifB* genes, retrieved from [Amoros et al. (2025)](https://journals.plos.org/plosgenetics/article?id=10.1371/journal.pgen.1011856) and representative of the 11 known *cif* types. These sequences are available on the article’s [GitHub page](https://github.com/julien01A/cif_evolution/tree/main/3.%20Screening%20of%20cifA%20and%20cifB%20and%20their%20predicted%20protein%20domains/cifA-cifB_sequences).
